06

Effect size & comparison

χ² grows with sample size; Cramér's V provides a dimension-adjusted description of association.

V = √[χ² / (N · min(r−1, c−1))]

07

Interpretation & limitations

Probability vs inference

Independence in probability requires P(X=i,F=j)=P(X=i)P(F=j) for all categories. A statistical test asks whether the observed differences are compatible with that model under its assumptions.

Association is not causality

A service/label association does not show that the service causes attacks. Dataset capture methodology, synthetic attack scenarios, routing and protocol choices are possible confounders.

Expected cell assumptions

The χ² approximation needs adequate expected frequencies. The dashboard warns if any expected cell is below 5; rare categories are grouped before testing, with their rows preserved.

Selection and dependence

UNSW-NB15 is a labelled cyber-range benchmark with generated attacks; successive flows can be dependent, so p-values should not be extrapolated to the open Internet or interpreted as direct real-world risk estimates.
